**Proposed label: State-specific curriculum guidance and operationalisation.** A shorter alternative is **Localised curriculum implementation guidance**.

The negative pole combines institutional and document vocabulary (*sed, dcr, orientativo, assessor, técnico-pedagógico, governança*) with terms for organising provision (*horas-aula, pós-médio, fic, ofertante, certificação, habilitação, sequenciado*). This suggests discourse that specifies how curricula are administered, scheduled and put into practice. Place names and contextual terms—including *teresina, manaus, goiânia, não-indígena, agronegócio* and *venezuelano*—also point to guidance grounded in particular state or local settings. The loadings are heterogeneous, so the pole should not be reduced to a single programme or educational stage.

The excerpts illustrate that range, though they are a limited, Tocantins-only sample. One assigns responsibilities for curriculum implementation and contains the loading words *governança* and *focal*; others set out evaluation indicators, teaching-hour arrangements and technical-professional pathways. Several excerpts have **no matched negative-pole loading words**, despite fitting that broader documentary setting. They therefore help qualify, rather than independently establish, the interpretation.

Tocantins has the lowest mean score (11.67), followed by Roraima (12.15), Rio de Janeiro (12.53) and Minas Gerais (12.66), making Tocantins the clearest state driver in the supplied examples. These states do not form a simple regional bloc; the tendency is better described as **state-specific** than as belonging to one Brazilian region. All reported means are positive, so “negative pole” here identifies the direction of the factor, not states with negative mean scores.
