### Proposed label: **Situated educational practice, care and institutional guidance**

The negative pole brings together vocabulary for the concrete organisation of education. Its strongest coherent strand concerns early-childhood care and experience: *fralda*, *refeição*, *balbucio* and *pré-escolar* sit alongside words for bodily action, play, materials and routines, such as *balançar*, *empilhar*, *utensílio* and *descanso*. Other loadings—including *emancipatória*, *não-indígena*, *técnico-pedagógico*, *governança* and *horas-aula*—broaden the dimension to questions of inclusion, educational organisation and institutional guidance. It is therefore not exclusively an early-childhood pole.

The Rio de Janeiro excerpts make the care-and-experience strand particularly legible. The loading words *fralda*, *refeição* and *balbucio* occur in guidance that treats care routines and children’s first attempts at communication as occasions for learning; *pré-escolar* occurs in an account of education as a right. Several other excerpts have **no identified loading words**. Their discussions of learning rights, pedagogical practice and curriculum implementation help qualify the discourse, but cannot by themselves establish its corpus-wide lexical character.

All reported state means are positive, so “negative pole” here means the **lowest relative factor scores**, not negative state averages. Rio de Janeiro is lowest (14.63) and supplies every example; Pernambuco, Rio Grande do Norte, Tocantins, Minas Gerais and Amazonas are also relatively low (about 16.6–17.0). This suggests a Rio de Janeiro-led tendency, not a consistent regional pattern: neighbouring or same-region states vary substantially.
