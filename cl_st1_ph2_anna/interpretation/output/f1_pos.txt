**Proposed label: Critical, multimodal curriculum articulation.** An alternative is **Reflective interdisciplinary curriculum design**.

The strongest loadings point to curriculum discourse that connects ways of making and interpreting meaning—*discursivo, multissemiótico, multiletramento, infográfico, reportagem, vídeo, digital*—with critical, theoretically framed learning: *problematização, dialético, argumentar, crítico, teórico-metodológico, análise* and *reflexivo*. Terms such as *ensino-aprendizagem, interdisciplinaridade, mobilizar* and *sistematização* give this a curricular function: they concern how knowledge and activities are organised for learning, not simply a list of media or school subjects.

The excerpts illustrate that function across different areas. The Santa Catarina passage on human and social sciences explicitly links *análise*, arguments and *competências* to questioning socially constructed realities, including socio-environmental degradation. The unit on identities and territorialities uses *compreensão, constitutivos, influência* and *pluralidade* while setting out learning about Indigenous peoples, diversity and public discourse. Other excerpts organise cross-area *trilhas* around youth, cultural production, technology or local environmental problems. The excerpts often show course structures rather than the media practices indicated by the highest loadings; their accompanying loading-word lists relate to the **full high-scoring texts**, so those practices should not be inferred from every displayed passage.
