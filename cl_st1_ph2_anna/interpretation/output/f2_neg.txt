### Factor f2 (negative): **Expressive practice and multimodal literacy**

This pole foregrounds curricula that treat learning as **making, interpreting and experimenting with forms of expression**. Across the full corpus, strongly loading terms such as *estilização*, *modalização*, *paródia*, *hipertexto*, *sintático* and *oração* point to attention to how language is constructed and reshaped. *Reportagem*, *narração*, *artístico-literário* and *lenda* extend that attention to genres; *câmera*, *infográfico*, *gestualidade*, *tinta* and *palco* bring digital, visual, material and bodily expression into view. The pole thus covers analysis as well as creative practice, rather than any single subject or medium.

The excerpts show how this discourse becomes pedagogical. Paraíba’s early-childhood guidance links *imitação* and *letra* to storytelling, interaction and exploratory mark-making; its theatre guidance uses *cena*, *artista* and *câmera* in proposals for performance, reflection and recording. Other passages make *game*, *celular* and *hipertexto* resources for participatory learning. These examples illustrate the broader lexical pattern, but do not exhaust it: a passage on curriculum coding also contains pole-loading words, so the factor should not be labelled simply “arts teaching” or “digital games.”

**Paraíba appears to drive the sampled pole**: it has the lowest state mean (4.40), and every supplied excerpt is from Paraíba. Alagoas (4.81) and Pernambuco (5.32) also have low means, followed by Rio Grande do Norte (5.51), Rio de Janeiro (5.57) and Mato Grosso (5.64). Several Northeastern states are therefore prominent among the lower means, but the region is not uniform; Bahia and Sergipe, for example, score much higher. All reported state means are positive, so “negative pole” here identifies the factor direction, not negative state means.

A shorter possible label is **“Creative, embodied and multimodal language learning.”**
