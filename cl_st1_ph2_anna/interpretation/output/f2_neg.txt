### Factor f2 (negative): **Subject-specific pedagogical explanation**

An alternative label is **Disciplinary concepts and teaching approaches**. This pole appears to mark curriculum passages that explain *how particular subjects are understood and taught*, rather than a discourse confined to one school subject.

The loadings span several disciplinary vocabularies: language and grammar (*gerúndio, infinitivo, modalização, oração*), arts education (*triangular, mae, pintura, estilização*), physical education and development (*esportivização, biopsicológico, gestualidade*), and mathematical or spatial concepts (*quadrado, espiral*). Terms such as *pcn* and *folclore* also suggest that some explanations draw on curricular frameworks and cultural traditions. This breadth is why a label based solely on mathematics, arts, or local culture would be too narrow.

The clearest negative-scoring excerpts illustrate the pattern in different subjects. Two Pernambuco passages explain the teaching of algebra and geometry through students’ developing understanding; their pole words are **quadrado** and **triangular**. Another traces conceptions of arts education and explains Ana Mae Barbosa’s *Abordagem Triangular*, matching **contemporânea, triangular** and **mae**. These examples support an explanatory, pedagogical reading, but cannot establish that every loaded term or subject is equally prominent across the corpus. The later excerpts score **+1** and report no matching negative-pole words, so they offer little direct evidence for this pole.

**Pernambuco appears to drive the illustrated pole**: it has the lowest state mean (4.66), and all supplied excerpts are from PE. Paraíba (4.98) and Alagoas (5.14) are the next-lowest means. This suggests a concentration among these three northeastern strata, **not** a uniform Northeast tendency: Bahia, Maranhão and Sergipe have substantially higher means. All state means are positive, so “negative pole” here means *relatively lower f2 scores*, not negative state averages.
