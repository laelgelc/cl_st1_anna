### Factor f2 positive: **Regulated provision of educational pathways and qualifications**

This pole encodes discourse about **putting educational provision into effect**: defining courses and curricular pathways, specifying who offers them, setting requirements, and establishing how participation and completion are recognised. Its strongest loadings—*certificação, habilitação, cursar, catálogo, frequentar, capacitação, carga, qualificação, ofertante, ofertar* and *curso*—give qualifications and course provision particular prominence. Alongside them, *portaria, órgão, normatizar, regulamentação, deliberação, legislação* and *diretriz* frame provision as something authorised and governed; *itinerário, horário, regime, estadual, municipal* and *rede* locate it in organised education systems.

The high-scoring Santa Catarina excerpts make those functions concrete. Passages on technical and professional itineraries specify *carga* horária, conditions for *oferta*, approval of a *curso*, and rules for *certificação* and *habilitação*, often citing a *portaria* or *diretriz*. Other excerpts apply similar language of legally grounded organisation to EJA, Indigenous schooling, full-time education and teacher preparation. The pole is therefore **not solely vocational**: the broader loading pattern and the varied examples point to the regulated organisation of access, curricula and credentials across educational modalities.

Santa Catarina is the clearest state-level driver, with the highest mean (**11.83**), and all supplied high-scoring examples come from it. Sergipe (**9.94**), Bahia (**9.76**), Mato Grosso do Sul (**9.41**) and Maranhão (**9.20**) also score strongly. Relatively high means occur across regions—including Rio Grande do Sul, Pará, Ceará and Piauí—so the tendency is not confined to one regional bloc. Because the illustrations are all from Santa Catarina, their detailed emphasis on *Novo Ensino Médio* itineraries should qualify, rather than define, the corpus-wide interpretation.

A slightly broader alternative label is **Legal-administrative organisation of educational provision**; the preferred label better retains the unusually strong emphasis on pathways, qualifications and certification.
