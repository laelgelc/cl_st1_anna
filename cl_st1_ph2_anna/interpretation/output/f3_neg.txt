The pole brings together language of curricular organisation—*competência, médio, tecnologias, juventude, horas-aula, DCN, DCR*—with terms for teaching across fields and modes, including *interdisciplinarmente, transversalmente, multissemiótico* and *multiletramento*. Loadings such as *Bloom, taxonomia, gerúndio, infinitivo, oração, coesão* and *espanhol* show that the full-corpus pattern also includes more specific assessment, language-teaching and content guidance. The label therefore describes a **way of framing and organising teaching**, not one subject or theme.

Some passages situate those choices in Amazonian cultures, sustainability and antiracist education; these qualify the discourse illustrated by the samples rather than defining every text at the pole.
