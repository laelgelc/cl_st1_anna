**Possible label for f3 (negative): “Curricular specification for inclusive and socially responsive education.”**

The loadings suggest a discourse that specifies *what* education should address and *how* it should be organised. Terms such as **gerúndio, infinitivo, oração, sintático, modalização, coesão** and **LP** point to detailed language-curriculum content; **horas-aula, progressividade, compartimentação** and **técnico-pedagógico** point to its sequencing and provision. Alongside these, **emancipatória, não-indígena, eugenia, desinformação** and **terreiro** indicate attention to social difference, historical harms and critical formation. The pole is therefore broader than either language instruction or inclusion alone.

The Pernambuco excerpts illustrate parts of this pattern. One defines the special-education population as **constituído** by specified groups and explains provision of support; another uses **LP** while detailing how language skills are coded and progressively organised. **Intencionar** occurs in a description of literary reading and reader formation. Other excerpts discuss citizenship and rights, but contain no identified negative-pole loading words, so they are weaker lexical evidence. Several excerpts also repeat the same Pernambuco passage and should not be counted as independent confirmation of its prominence.

**Pernambuco** has the lowest state mean (4.43) and supplies all the examples; **Minas Gerais** is virtually tied (4.45), followed by **Piauí** (4.92). This suggests a relative tendency across more than one state, not a clear regional concentration. All state means are positive, so “negative pole” here denotes the *lowest relative scores*, not negative state averages.
