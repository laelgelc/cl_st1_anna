### Factor f3 (positive): **Playful, sensory and caring early-childhood pedagogy**

This pole encodes discourse about how babies and young children learn through **play, bodily and sensory exploration, interaction and everyday care**, with adults organising environments and responding to children’s interests. Its strongest loadings—*brinquedo, textura, manusear, imitar, bebê, areia, brincar, conversar* and *higiene*—locate it firmly in early-childhood practice. Further loadings such as *professora, afeto, cuidado, gesto, som, creche, pré-escola* and *rotina* connect those activities to relationships and institutional care. The pole is therefore broader than a discourse of play alone.

The high-scoring excerpts illustrate that breadth. In the Rio de Janeiro texts, children *manipular* objects and explore *textura* and *som*; *brincar* and *brincadeira* support movement, language, investigation and artistic expression. The *professora* listens, *acolhe* and helps children develop ideas, while spaces, materials and routines—including *higiene*, *cuidado* and access to the *pátio*—are treated as part of pedagogy. The excerpts on literacy and nature show applications of this approach, not separate themes that define the whole pole.

**Rio de Janeiro** has the highest state mean (10.02) and supplies all the examples, so it is the clearest illustrated contributor. **Acre** (9.75), **Amazonas** (9.30) and **Goiás** (8.87) also score strongly, followed by **Ceará**, **Santa Catarina** and **Mato Grosso**. The elevated means span several regions rather than forming a single regional bloc; the Rio examples cannot establish what the higher-scoring texts in those other states specifically say.

Other concise labels would be **“Experiential early-childhood learning and care”** or **“Mediated play and sensory exploration in early childhood.”**
