## Factor f3 (positive): proposed label

**Play-based, embodied early-childhood pedagogy**

This pole encodes guidance on how babies and young children learn through **playing, moving, sensing, handling materials and interacting with others**, with adults arranging and supporting those experiences. The strongest loadings are notably concrete: *empilhar*, *bater*, *imitar*, *textura*, *bebê*, *brinquedo*, *manusear*, *encaixe* and *esconder*. Other loadings connect these actions to bodily expression (*gesto*, *balbucio*, *som*, *dramatizar*), everyday care (*higiene*, *fralda*, *refeição*, *colo*, *descanso*) and pedagogical settings (*creche*, *espaços*, *professora*, *rotina*). Together, they suggest a discourse of organising lived experiences rather than a single curriculum subject.

The high-scoring excerpts illustrate that breadth. Rio de Janeiro texts describe exploration of objects and *textura*, bodily *gesto* and *brincadeira*, a *professora* who supports children’s questions, and spaces equipped with *brinquedo* and *mobiliário*. Others extend the same experiential approach to conversation and *balbucio*, nature and *areia*, artistic expression, and early encounters with writing. These are varied applications of the loading pattern, not evidence that any one topic defines the whole pole.

**Rio de Janeiro drives the supplied examples** and has the highest state mean (6.44). Acre (5.88) and Amazonas (5.38) also score strongly, followed by Goiás (4.19); the means therefore suggest a wider, uneven distribution rather than an exclusively Rio de Janeiro discourse. The Rio excerpts show particularly clearly how this pole turns early-childhood principles into situated practices of play, exploration, care and adult mediation.
