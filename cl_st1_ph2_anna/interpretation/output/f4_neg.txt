### Factor f4 (negative): **Guidance for staged and monitored learning**

This pole appears to encode curriculum guidance that specifies **how learning should be developed, checked and adjusted over time**. Its loadings combine assessment and monitoring terms—*somativo, autoavaliação, diagnosticar, verificação, mensurar, portfólio* and *tutoria*—with terms for sequencing and method, including *progressividade, gradual, cronograma, recomendável* and *metódico*. Mathematical and technical terms such as *calculador, fórmula, proporcionalidade, quadrado* and *estimativa* show that this guidance can be expressed through detailed, subject-specific teaching advice. Other loadings, including *protótipo, simulação, certificação* and *carreira*, suggest applications beyond mathematics.

The excerpts illustrate one particularly clear form of the discourse: Pernambuco’s mathematics guidance recommends introducing concepts gradually, building on students’ existing understandings and using problems, estimates and calculators purposefully. The loading words *recomendável, gradual, calculador, proporcionalidade, fórmula* and *quadrado* occur in advice about when and how to teach, not simply in lists of content. In the assessment excerpts, *autoavaliação, assertivo* and *verificação* accompany guidance on using evidence of learning to revise teaching. Importantly, *somativo* occurs in a classification of assessment types; its loading does not mean that the pole advocates summative testing.

**Pernambuco is the clearest state driver in the supplied evidence**: it has the lowest mean score (1.23), and all ten illustrative excerpts come from it, with some closely related or repeated material. The next-lowest means are Paraíba (1.45) and São Paulo (1.47), followed by Acre (1.65) and Amapá (1.73). All state means are positive, so these are *relatively* stronger associations with the negative pole, not negative state averages. Their distribution across regions does not support a simple regional attribution. The exclusively Pernambuco examples make its mathematics-and-assessment formulation especially visible, while the full loading list indicates a broader discourse of sequenced instruction, tools and evaluation.

A concise alternative label is **“Procedural guidance for learning and assessment.”**
