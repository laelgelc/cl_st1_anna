### Factor f5 (positive): **Specification of language practices and learning progressions**

This pole appears to encode discourse that **names, differentiates and organises what learners do with languages, texts and other forms of expression**, often spelling out how those practices develop across a curriculum. “Multimodal literacy and curricular specification” is a shorter alternative label.

The loadings support both parts of that interpretation. Terms such as *modalização*, *estilização*, *ortografia*, *sintático*, *oração* and *verbo* point to explicit treatment of linguistic form. *Reportagem*, *infográfico*, *hipertexto*, *multissemiótico*, *multiletramento* and *letramento* identify genres and modes of communication; *baixar*, *celular*, *edição* and *imprimir* add concrete media practices. Meanwhile, *sequência*, *progressão*, *sequencial*, *coluna* and *sigla* suggest the specification and ordering of curricular content.

The high-scoring Goiás excerpts illustrate this range rather than a single topic. One explains digital terms including *game* and *sequência*; another links *hipertexto*, *multissemiótico* and *multiletramento* to reading and production. Several explain curricular codes and horizontal or vertical *progressão*, using *sigla*, *letra* and *sequencial*. Early-childhood excerpts broaden the pattern to artistic expression, language development and investigation, so **digital literacy alone would be too narrow a label**.

Goiás has the highest mean (2.25) and supplies all the examples, making it the clearest visible driver. Tocantins is close (2.23), though highly variable, followed by Santa Catarina (2.01), Alagoas (1.88) and Paraná (1.79). The elevated scores span regions rather than indicating an exclusively regional discourse; the all-Goiás examples cannot establish how other high-scoring states realise the pole.
