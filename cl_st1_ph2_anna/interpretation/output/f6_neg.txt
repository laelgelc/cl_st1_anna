### Factor f6 (negative): proposed label
**Contextualised curricular-pedagogical elaboration**

The negative loadings suggest discourse that elaborates curriculum through specialised educational concepts and institutional settings. They span theories of learning and development (*sócio-histórico, histórico-crítico, gnosiológico, assimilação*), particular teaching domains (*ortografia, sintático, codificação, desporto, esportivização, gestualidade*), and the organisation or location of education systems (*municípios, dirigente, secretário, distrital, manaus*). This is a heterogeneous vocabulary, so the label describes a broad discourse function rather than a single subject area. Proper names and the unusually prominent *go* also caution against treating every loading as a substantive theme.

**Rio de Janeiro appears to drive the sampled pole**: it has the lowest state mean (1.79), and every supplied excerpt is from RJ. Amazonas (1.99), Pernambuco (2.13), Minas Gerais (2.15) and Rio Grande do Norte (2.18) also have relatively low means. These states do not form a clear regional bloc.

The RJ excerpts show what such elaboration can do in practice: they explain early-childhood learning through social interaction and play, qualify assessment and transitions between school stages, and describe participatory curriculum review and adaptation to local populations. The physical-education excerpt similarly situates bodily practices in cultural and pedagogical terms. **The lexical overlap is limited**, however: only *codificação* is reported as a negative-pole loading word in an excerpt; the other nine report none. Thus, childhood pedagogy and participatory review are illustrative RJ realisations, not grounds for renaming the entire pole after those topics.

A slightly broader alternative label is **“Situated educational guidance.”**
