**Proposed label: “Planning formative pathways for inquiry and action.”**

This positive pole combines the **organisation of curriculum pathways** with the **abilities students are expected to develop through them**. Across the loadings, *trilha, estruturante, matriz, perfil, semanal* and *cronograma* point to the design and delivery of learning opportunities. *Processar, testar, argumento, respaldar, problema* and *tecnologia* point to inquiry and evidence-based problem-solving; *mediação, sustentabilidade* and *ambiental* extend that work to sociocultural and environmental action. The strongly loading *perseverar, coragem, persistência, estresse* and *adversidade*, alongside *empreendedorismo*, add a discourse of initiative and resilience.

The excerpts show how these strands meet, without each excerpt displaying the whole pattern. The Santa Catarina pathway descriptions specify teaching profiles, weekly lessons and curricular matrices (001–002, 004), while the account of science pathways links *trilha*, *estruturante*, *mediação*, *empreendedorismo*, *problema* and *sustentabilidade* to integrated, progressively challenging learning (003). Most explicitly, the skills table (009) brings together scientific inquiry, creative processes, sociocultural intervention and entrepreneurial perseverance—the source of several of the pole’s strongest loading words.

**Santa Catarina clearly drives the sampled high end**: its mean is 9.97, far above the next-highest, Paraná at 3.81. The Paraná score offers some support for a southern presence, but Rio Grande do Sul’s 2.48 cautions against treating this as a uniform regional tendency. The label therefore captures a corpus-wide lexical pattern that is especially concentrated in the Santa Catarina excerpts, rather than treating their particular pathway format as characteristic of every state.
